Last updated: 25 May 2026

Sono is a picture-symbol communication app designed to help people express themselves using images and spoken words.

3. How We Use This Information

The information listed above is used solely to operate the app on your device:

  • Display and board settings — adapt the communication board to the user’s needs.
  • Voice selection — drives the text-to-speech engine.
  • On-device language model files — when present and enabled, used solely as input to the local sentence-suggestion engine (see Section 5).
  • Custom content and media — displays personalised symbols, photos, labels, and voice recordings.
  • Activity history — supports recents, message history, and faster re-use of common communication.

None of this information is used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app locally on your device.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

The App stores the data in Section 2 on the device under the user’s or caretaker’s control. We do not receive that data through ordinary App use. Choosing a setting, adding content, or importing a model is a feature choice; it is not, by itself, GDPR consent to processing by us. We therefore do not assign an Article 6 legal basis to each on-device item as though we received and controlled it.

When you contact us, we process the personal information you provide to respond to your enquiry. Where GDPR applies, our basis is our legitimate interest in handling enquiries (Article 6(1)(f)); for a request to exercise data protection rights, we process the information needed to meet our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Optional requests to external hosts are described in Sections 7, 10, and 11; those hosts provide information about their own processing.

The App does not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on an individual (see Section 13).

5. Sentence Suggestions and On-Device AI

Sono can offer suggestions to help turn a sequence of selected symbols into a more natural sentence. The caretaker can choose between two engines in Settings > Smart Sentences:

Rule-based engine (default): A deterministic, local grammar engine that applies fixed rules to the symbol sequence. It does not use machine learning, does not transmit anything externally, and produces no profile or inference about any individual.

On-device language model (optional, caretaker-supplied): Some builds of the App include a “Bring Your Own Model” path that allows the caretaker to import a GGUF-format language model file from device storage or fetch one from a URL the caretaker chooses, and use it to generate sentence suggestions locally. No language model is bundled with the App. Whether this feature is available, and which on-device inference engine (if any) is used to run the imported model, depends on how the build is configured by the publisher or by the caretaker.

Where this feature is available and the caretaker has imported a model:

  • The selected symbol sequence (in plain text form) is provided as input to the imported model only when sentence suggestion is invoked, and only on your device.
  • No prompt, no input, no model output, and no other communication content is transmitted to us or to any external service when a suggestion is generated.
  • Model files are imported or downloaded at the caretaker’s explicit request, from a location the caretaker chooses (see Section 7 and Section 10). The App does not recommend, host, or pre-select any particular model file or vendor. Once imported, the model runs locally.
  • The model produces only short text suggestions for the caretaker or user to accept, reject, or edit. It does not perform profiling or automated decision-making within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR, and its output has no legal or similarly significant effect on any individual.
  • You are informed here, and via the Smart Sentences setting itself, that suggestions originate from an AI system. This is intended to support the transparency obligations for AI systems that interact with natural persons under Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the “EU AI Act”), to the extent applicable to this feature. The upstream model provider may have separate obligations for its model; we remain responsible for any obligations applicable law assigns to us.

If you turn the on-device language model off, or do not import any model file, no language-model processing takes place. Imported model files can be deleted in Settings > Smart Sentences at any time.

6. Text-to-Speech

Sono offers two text-to-speech modes:

Offline voices (preferred): The app can download language-specific voice models from a public repository (the Sherpa-ONNX project’s GitHub releases). The synthesis engine is built on the open-source sherpa_onnx library, which runs entirely on the device via ONNX Runtime. Once a voice model is downloaded, all speech synthesis happens locally with no data transmitted externally.

Caretaker-provided voices (“Bring Your Own Voice”): The caretaker may optionally import voice model files from a folder or download link, or record short audio samples for individual symbols. Imported voice model files, generated voices, and per-symbol voice recordings are stored only on the device and are never transmitted by the App. The caretaker is responsible for obtaining any consent required by applicable law for recording, storing, importing, or sharing another person’s voice (see Section 7 of the Terms and Conditions).

Device system voices (fallback): When an offline voice is not available for the selected language, the app uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech service (provided by Apple or Google, depending on your device). These system services are governed by the privacy policies of Apple and Google respectively, and may transmit spoken text to their servers to synthesise it. You can avoid this by downloading an offline voice in Settings > Voice.

7. Optional Internet Connections

Sono has no mandatory internet connection for core communication. The communication board, sentence strip, and text-to-speech using an installed offline voice operate offline after installation. Device system voices used as a fallback may process text under the device provider’s terms, as explained in Section 6.

The following network activity may occur, depending on caretaker choices and on how the build is configured:

  • Offline voice models — downloaded from Sherpa-ONNX releases on GitHub (github.com) when you select and download a voice in Settings. Model size varies by language.
  • On-device language model files — where the Smart Sentences “Bring Your Own Model” path is available and the caretaker chooses to download a model, the file is fetched over HTTPS from the URL the caretaker provides. The App does not pre-configure any particular model URL and does not embed credentials, account identifiers, or telemetry in this download.
  • Custom caretaker-provided voice files — when the caretaker chooses to import a voice model from a folder or download link, the corresponding file is fetched over HTTPS from the URL the caretaker provides.
  • Remote lesson catalog and content — the App fetches a lesson manifest list, individual lesson JSON files, and scene images over HTTPS from its configured lessons media host (the default is https://ljmatan.github.io/apps/communicator; builds may override it at compile time with GCM_LESSONS_BASE_URL). This happens as the caretaker or user opens the Learning area. Downloaded lesson content is cached locally for offline replay; lesson progress is stored on the device and is not transmitted back to us.
  • Legal documents — opened from the document URLs configured in the App. Where the App is configured to fetch legal documents from a remote URL, those requests are made to the hosting service identified in Section 10.

These requests transfer the data inherent to an HTTPS request, such as your IP address, basic request headers, and the URL path of the resource being fetched. No communication content, custom content, or usage data is included in these requests. Once a resource is downloaded or cached, the associated feature operates locally.

The treatment of HTTPS request metadata under GDPR is addressed in Section 11.

Because the app does not transmit communication content to our servers and stores communication content locally on the device, it presents a reduced privacy risk for users, including children.

EU/EEA — Children’s consent (GDPR Article 8): Article 8 applies when an information society service is offered directly to a child and the processing relies on the child’s consent under Article 6(1)(a). The relevant age varies by member state. This policy does not treat a caretaker’s creation of local custom symbols, labels, photos, or recordings as GDPR consent given to us. Parents and guardians should review how the device and any selected external services handle a child’s information and obtain any permissions required for media involving other people.

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Activity history: Recent symbols and message history are retained locally for quick re-use until cleared through app controls, cleared by the consent/storage system where applicable, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

Preference data (display, board, speech, and behaviour settings): Retained on the device until the caretaker changes the setting manually, clears app data, or uninstalls the app.

Custom content and media: Custom symbols, categories, labels, photos, and voice recordings are retained on the device until individually removed in the app, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

TTS model files: Retained on the device until deleted via Settings, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

On-device language model files: Retained on the device until deleted via Settings > Smart Sentences, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. No record of inputs supplied to the model, or outputs produced by it, is retained after the suggestion has been displayed.

Lesson cache (when the remote lessons backend is configured): Lesson manifests, lesson JSON, and scene images downloaded for offline replay are retained on the device until cleared through app controls, replaced by an updated version on next fetch, or removed when the App is uninstalled. Lesson progress (which lessons or scenes have been viewed) is retained on the device until cleared or uninstall.

Caretaker backup archives: A backup archive (.sonobkp) is created only on demand at the caretaker’s request and written to a location the caretaker chooses. The App does not retain a copy of the archive. Retention and deletion of the archive are entirely under the caretaker’s control.

To delete all app data: Uninstalling the app from your device removes locally stored files, including preferences, custom symbols, custom labels, custom photos, custom voice recordings, activity history, downloaded voice model files, downloaded on-device language model files, and the local lesson cache. Backup archives written outside the App’s sandbox (for example to the device’s Files app, Photos library, or a cloud sync folder) are not removed by uninstalling the App.

We do not receive or retain the App content described above through ordinary App use. Information you voluntarily send us in an enquiry is processed as described in Section 4.

Enquiries and rights requests: We keep information you send us directly for as long as reasonably needed to answer and handle follow-up, and longer where a legal obligation or claim requires it.

16. Data Security

All data stored by the app resides in the app’s private sandboxed container on your device, which is protected by your operating system’s built-in security mechanisms (file-system encryption, process isolation, app sandboxing). The app does not implement encryption layers beyond what the operating system provides.

The App does not transmit communication content to our infrastructure during ordinary use. This does not rule out a personal data breach involving information we control, such as correspondence sent to us, or a security issue affecting data stored on a user’s device. If a breach of personal data for which we are controller occurs, we will assess it and notify the competent supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to individuals’ rights and freedoms (GDPR Article 33). We will inform affected individuals when Article 34 requires it.

17. Backup, Export, and Import

The App includes a backup feature that lets the caretaker save and restore the App’s customised data. The feature is initiated only by the caretaker, from Caretaker Settings, and is never triggered automatically.

What the backup contains. A backup archive (.sonobkp — a standard ZIP container) bundles:

  • manifest.json — backup format version and the date the archive was created;
  • data.json — caretaker-managed JSON entries such as the custom symbols index, custom categories, custom images index, voiceovers index, hidden symbols and categories, quick phrases, intent starters, grid size, display style, board layout, user gender preference, double-tap-to-add setting, kiosk mode setting, primary colour, and the selected TTS voice;
  • files/ — every custom media file referenced by those entries, including custom symbol images, custom category images, custom photo files, and custom voice recordings.

It does not contain TTS voice model files, on-device language model files, or any data that did not originate from the caretaker’s own customisation.

Where the backup is stored. The archive is written to a temporary file inside the App’s sandbox and then offered to the caretaker through the platform share or save sheet. Once the caretaker chooses a destination (for example the device’s Files app, Photos library, a cloud sync folder, or another app), the privacy practices of that destination also apply to the copy stored there. The App does not retain a copy of the archive and does not transmit the archive to us.

Import behaviour. When a caretaker imports a .sonobkp archive, the App overwrites the corresponding caretaker-managed entries and replaces the matching custom media files on the device. Other settings (such as on-device language model files and TTS voice models) are not touched.

Caretaker responsibility. Because a backup contains custom photos and voice recordings — which may identify the user or third parties — the caretaker is responsible for:

  • Protecting the backup file against unauthorised access (for example by storing it in a protected location);
  • Confirming they have the rights or consents needed to export or import any media that identifies another person;
  • Deleting backup files that are no longer needed.

The corresponding terms and caretaker obligations are set out in Section 7 of the Terms and Conditions.
