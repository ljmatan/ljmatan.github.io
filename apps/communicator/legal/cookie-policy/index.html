Cookie and Local Storage Policy — Sono AAC Communicator

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website or web-based service to store information between sessions. They are a web browser technology and are not inherently part of native mobile applications.

2. Does This App Use Cookies?

Mostly no. Sono is a native mobile application. During normal use — browsing symbols, building sentences, playing voice output, recording optional custom voice messages, or using local sentence polishing — no web browser cookies, web sessions, or browser storage are used.

The App includes an embedded WebView component used only to display legal documents (this Cookie Policy, the Privacy Policy, and the Terms and Conditions) when you choose to view them. Depending on how those documents are hosted, that WebView may or may not transmit cookies to your device (see Section 4).

There are no tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies used at any point during normal operation of the App.

3. Local Storage Used by the App

Although the App does not use browser cookies during normal operation, it does store app settings and user-generated communication content on your device using the App’s internal cache and file storage. These local entries are used to operate the App and do not track you across applications or devices.

Under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC), storing information on, or accessing information already stored on, the user’s terminal equipment generally requires consent unless the storage or access is strictly necessary to provide the service the user has explicitly requested. The classification below maps each local-storage item to one of those two categories. We use the term “mandatory storage” for strictly necessary entries and “functional storage” for entries that depend on caretaker preference. Neither category uses HTTP cookies.

4. Embedded WebView for Legal Document Display

The App includes an embedded WebView component (powered by webview_flutter) used exclusively to display legal documents — this Cookie Policy, the Privacy Policy, and the Terms and Conditions. This WebView is opened only when you deliberately tap a legal document link on the App’s welcome screen.

If the legal documents are hosted at a remote URL: The server delivering those documents may set cookies or use other tracking mechanisms in the WebView, in the same way as any website visited in a browser. The hosting service’s privacy and cookie notices describe its own practices. This policy still describes the App’s use of the WebView and local storage; the Privacy Policy describes any processing for which we are responsible.

If the legal documents are served from local App assets: No external network request is made, and no cookies are set or received.

In the published App: The legal documents open from the Sono website, which sets no cookies and uses no browser storage (see Section 10).

No other WebView component, embedded browser, or third-party web content is loaded during any other part of the App’s normal operation.

5. Third-Party Services and Their Own Storage

When you voluntarily download optional TTS model files, import a caretaker-selected on-device language model, fetch remote lessons (where the build is configured with a lessons backend), or open externally hosted legal documents, the App makes HTTPS requests to third-party servers. These servers may set their own cookies or use their own tracking mechanisms if you subsequently visit them in a browser, but they do not set browser cookies on your device through the App’s native interface.

6. Backup Archives Created by the App

The App includes a backup feature that lets the caretaker export and re-import all caretaker-managed content from Caretaker Settings. The feature is initiated only by the caretaker.

When an export runs, the App temporarily writes a .sonobkp ZIP archive inside its own sandbox and then offers the archive to the caretaker via the platform share or save sheet. The archive contains:

  • manifest.json — backup format version and the date of export;
  • data.json — caretaker-managed JSON entries (custom symbols, custom categories, custom images index, voiceovers index, hidden items, quick phrases, intent starters, grid/display/board/colour settings, and the selected TTS voice);
  • files/ — every custom media file referenced by those entries, including custom symbol images, custom category images, custom photo files, and custom voice recordings.

The archive does not contain TTS voice model files, on-device language model files, or anything that did not originate from caretaker customisation.

Once the archive leaves the App’s sandbox (for example because the caretaker saves it to the Files app, the Photos library, a cloud sync folder, or another app), the caretaker is responsible for its protection, retention, and deletion. The privacy and cookie/local-storage practices of the destination application or service apply to the archive at that point — not this policy.

For privacy implications and caretaker responsibilities around backups, see Section 17 of the Privacy Policy.

7. Removing All App Storage

You can remove all data stored by the App at any time by uninstalling the App from your device. This will permanently delete preference files, custom symbols, custom photos, custom voice recordings, message history, downloaded voice model files, the lesson cache, and any caretaker-imported on-device language model files. The process varies slightly by platform:

iOS: Go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) > Sono > Delete App.

Android: Go to Settings > Apps > Sono > Uninstall. Alternatively, use Settings > Apps > Sono > Storage > Clear Data to remove all stored data without uninstalling the App.

You can also remove individual custom photos, custom voice recordings, the lesson cache, the on-device language model index, and other custom symbol content from the relevant app controls.

Backup archives that the caretaker has saved outside the App’s sandbox (for example to the Files app, Photos library, or a cloud sync folder) are not removed by uninstalling the App. The caretaker is responsible for deleting those archives from their destination.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie and Local Storage Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision. Because this App does not use browser cookies and its local storage practices change only with significant App updates, changes to this policy are expected to be infrequent.

10. The Sono Website

The Sono website at sono.click and ljmatan.github.io/apps/communicator sets no cookies, neither its own nor third-party cookies, and does not use local storage, session storage or similar browser storage. It has no analytics, advertising or embedded third-party content, and it serves its fonts, images and scripts itself.

Apart from the copies of pages and files that your browser caches on its own, the website stores nothing on your device and reads nothing from it. Under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive there is therefore nothing to ask consent for, and the website shows no cookie banner.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. What GitHub receives and logs when you visit is described in Section 21 of the Privacy Policy.

If we ever add something that stores or reads information on your device, such as analytics or embedded videos, we will update this section first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before anything is stored.

This Cookie and Local Storage Policy applies to the Sono native mobile app and, as described in Section 10, to the Sono website. It does not apply to other websites or web-based services.
