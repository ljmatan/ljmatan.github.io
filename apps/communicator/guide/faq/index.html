Usage guide

FAQ

Answers to the questions caretakers most often ask when first setting up the app.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The full symbol board, speech engine, and all activities work entirely on the device. No internet connection is required for day-to-day use.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Yes — install it on as many devices as you need. Settings, hidden symbols, recents and custom photos are stored on each device individually.

What age is the app suitable for?

There’s no fixed age range. The app is designed for any person who benefits from picture-based communication — toddlers, children, teenagers and adults. Adjust grid size and display style to match the communicator’s reading and motor ability.

Which languages are supported?

Sono is available in 11 languages: English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Croatian, Czech, Polish, Finnish, Slovak and Danish. The language follows the voice you choose: pick one when you first set up Sono, or later in Caretaker Settings > Speech & Voice, and the interface and symbol names switch with it. English has a US and a UK voice. Every language except Croatian has an offline voice you download once. Until you do, and always for Croatian, Sono uses the device's own text-to-speech, which needs that language installed on the device.
