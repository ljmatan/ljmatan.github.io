Usage guide

Building sentences

What happens on the sentence strip in the Advanced and Menu layouts: adding words, speaking, searching, quick phrases and drawing.

Building a sentence

The sentence strip sits at the bottom of the screen in the Advanced and Menu layouts. By default one tap speaks a symbol and a double tap adds it to the strip; under Caretaker Settings > Board Display > Tap Behavior a single tap can add it instead. You can also press and hold a symbol and drag it onto the strip. Once there is a word on the strip, three buttons appear on the right: Delete removes the last word, Clear empties the strip and Speak says the whole sentence. Press and hold a word on the strip to remove just that word, or tap it to see the sentence full-screen with a large Speak button. In the Menu layout, the category page closes back to the main menu as soon as a word is added, ready for the next one. The Simple layout has no sentence strip: every tap simply speaks that word.

Speaking a sentence

Tap Speak and the sentence is read out with the chosen voice. A banner shows the whole message for a few seconds, the sentence is saved to Message History in Caretaker Settings, and the strip clears so the next sentence can start straight away. Speak does nothing while the strip is empty. When a symbol has a recorded voice (see Custom symbols), that recording plays instead of the synthetic voice whenever the symbol is tapped.

Search, Quick and Draw

While the strip is empty it shows four buttons: the settings gear, Search, Quick and Draw. • Search opens a search box: type part of a word and tap a result to add that symbol to the sentence. • Quick (the lightning bolt) opens a row of ready phrases that are spoken immediately, without touching the sentence: Hi, Bye, Yes, No, Please, Thank you, Sorry, Help, Stop, More, Wait, I love you, I'm fine, I don't know, Excuse me and Come here. Switch phrases off, or add any symbol from the library, under Caretaker Settings > Quick Phrases. • Draw opens a drawing board with six colours, three pen sizes, an eraser and undo, handy for a quick sketch of what words can't say. Drawings aren't saved. On tablets the buttons carry labels; on phones they are icons only.
