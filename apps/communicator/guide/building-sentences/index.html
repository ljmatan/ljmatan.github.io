Usage guide

Building sentences

Everything that happens on the sentence strip — adding words, speaking, clearing, removing.

Building a sentence

Tap any symbol on the board to add it to the sentence strip at the top of the screen. Symbols appear in order from left to right. Use the backspace button to remove the last word, or the clear button to start over.

Speaking a sentence

Once symbols are added to the sentence strip, tap the green speaker button to "speak" the sentence. A banner will display the full message. The sentence is saved to history and the strip clears automatically so a new sentence can be started right away.

Learning by example

The best way to teach someone to use the app is to use it yourself. Show how it works by tapping symbols alongside your normal speech: • Point to symbols on the app as you say the corresponding words. • Build short 1–3 word phrases on the sentence strip during conversation. • Do NOT require the individual to imitate you immediately. • Model throughout daily routines, not just during dedicated practice. The more often you demonstrate using the app in everyday moments, the faster the person will start using it independently. Aim to show at least 20–30 examples across the day.
