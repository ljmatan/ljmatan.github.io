Usage guide

Caretaker settings

The settings gear, grid size and symbol style, layouts and tap behaviour, kiosk mode, backups, printing and the voice.

On this page

  1. Opening settings
  2. Grid size and symbol style
  3. Board layouts and tap behaviour
  4. Message history and Recents

  6. Backups, printing and the voice

Opening settings # — Copy link to this section

Caretaker Settings are kept out of the person's way. Tap the gear, the first button on the sentence strip in the Advanced and Menu layouts, or the small gear at the start of the category row in the Simple layout, and answer a short addition question. It's there to confirm that a grown-up is opening the settings.

Grid size and symbol style # — Copy link to this section

Under Caretaker Settings > Board Display, Grid Size sets how many columns the board shows, from XS (2 columns) to XXXL (8). Fewer columns mean bigger buttons that are easier to see and tap: a good starting point for small children, for people with motor difficulties and for anyone who tires quickly. More columns show more symbols at once. Display Style switches the tiles between Card and Circle.

On first setup Sono picks 3 columns and circles on phones, and 5 columns and cards on large tablets. Change them any time without losing anything.

Board layouts and tap behaviour # — Copy link to this section

Caretaker Settings > Board Display > Board Layout offers three layouts, with a live preview of each

  • Advanced: the full board with category tabs, subcategory chips and the sentence strip. For people who join words into sentences.
  • Simple: category tabs and symbols only, no sentence strip. One tap, one word. The quietest screen, and the easiest for a first start, for one-word turn-taking and for modelling.
  • Menu: one category at a time with big tiles, and a sentence strip. For people who tire with dense grids or who tap imprecisely.

A new install starts in Menu. Tap Behavior decides whether a double tap (the default) or a single tap adds a word to the sentence; with the single-tap setting every tap adds, so repeated taps add the word repeatedly.

Under Caretaker Settings > Personalisation you can also show all text in uppercase and change the accent colour, the appearance and the picture style of the people illustrations.

Message history and Recents # — Copy link to this section

Caretaker Settings > Message History lists the last 20 sentences spoken from the strip, newest first, and shows the Recents symbols. Both can be cleared here. Reading the history in the evening shows which words the person reached for, and what you might model next.

Kiosk mode # — Copy link to this section

Caretaker Settings > Board Display > Kiosk Mode keeps the screen awake while the board is open. On Android it also shows the app on the lock screen and offers Pin App to Screen, which keeps the device in Sono until you unpin it (Android forgets the pinning after a restart); the same screen can make Sono the home screen app. On iPhone and iPad, use the system's Guided Access to lock the device to Sono. On computers the switch only keeps the screen awake.

Backups, printing and the voice # — Copy link to this section

  • Backup & Restore exports a .sonobkp file with your custom symbols, photos, recordings, hidden items, quick phrases, sentence starters and board settings. Share it to another device or keep it safe. Importing replaces the custom data on the device; message history and Recents are not included.
  • Print Boards makes an A4 PDF of one category or of all of them, five symbols per row, using your photos where you added them: a paper backup for the bath, the pool or a flat battery.
  • Speech & Voice is where the voice, and with it the app language, is chosen. See the FAQ for offline voices.
