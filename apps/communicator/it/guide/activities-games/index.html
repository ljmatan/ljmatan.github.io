Guida all’uso

Attività e giochi

Mini-giochi e attività integrati per linguaggio, ascolto, numeri e gioco.

Mini attività

Beyond the main board, the app includes a set of short activities for learning and play: • Math — simple addition, subtraction, and counting. • Spelling — building short words from letter tiles. • Comparison & sorting — odd-one-out, categories, more vs. less. • Listening — hear a word, choose the matching symbol. • Memory & flashcards — recognise and recall vocabulary. • Whiteboard — free-form drawing and mark-making. Use them as short brain breaks between communication sessions, or as a way to build specific skills.

Tocco e coordinazione

If the individual has motor challenges, consider these adjustments: • Use the fewest columns (3) for the largest tap targets. • Position the device at a comfortable angle — a tablet stand or wheelchair mount may help. • Allow extra response time (at least 5–10 seconds) before assuming the person has finished. • Watch for fatigue — shorter, more frequent sessions may be better than one long session. • If direct touch is difficult, try a stylus or explore other ways to interact with the screen. Grid size can be adjusted any time in Caretaker Settings without losing vocabulary or history.
