Sono AAC Communicator

Sono is a symbol-based communication app for people who cannot speak. Tap picture cards to build sentences — the app speaks the message aloud, including offline when an offline voice is installed.

Google Play Explore lessons

What it does

Everything a communicator needs

ABC

A rich symbol vocabulary

Hand-illustrated symbols organised into core words, people, actions, food, places, animals, time, numbers, ABC, and more — with subcategories and a search button for any symbol instantly.

FUN

Games and a drawing board

Spelling, maths, memory, listening and sorting games, plus a whiteboard for free drawing — playful practice that builds language skills.

VO

Offline neural voices

Optional offline voices can read sentences aloud without Wi-Fi after download. Voice availability and download size vary by language and device.

IMG

Custom photos on any symbol

Replace any symbol card with a photo from the camera or gallery. Familiar faces and objects make communication faster and more personal for the user.

LNG

Fully multilingual

Full support for English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Croatian, Czech, Polish, Finnish, Slovak, and Danish — interface text, symbol names, voices, and all legal documents.

OFF

Private by design

No account or in-app analytics. Communication content is stored on the device. Optional downloads and device system voices may contact external services, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

Getting started

Simple to use from day one

The caretaker sets the app up once. After that, the board is the only screen the user ever needs.

1

Set up the profile

Enter the user's display name and choose a preferred voice. This takes under two minutes and is hidden behind a caretaker lock.

2

Tap symbols to build a sentence

Browse by category tab, filter by subcategory, or use the search button to find any symbol instantly. Each tap also speaks the word aloud. Long-press a chip in the sentence bar to remove a single word.

3

Press speak — the app does the rest

The voice reads the whole sentence aloud, and a banner confirms what was spoken for 4 seconds.

Fit check

Who Sono is — and is not — for

✓

Sono works well for

  • Children and adults who do not use speech as their main way to communicate
  • Families and caretakers looking for a free, private communication tool
  • Educators and SLPs introducing symbol-based communication for the first time
  • Anyone who needs offline, on-device communication without an account
—

Sono is not designed for

  • Eye-gaze or switch scanning — for now Sono works with touch, a keyboard or TalkBack
  • Cloud syncing across multiple devices
  • Medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or replacing a clinician-prescribed communication system without guidance
  • iOS — Sono is currently Android-only

Privacy architecture

How private is private?

Sono stores communication content locally. Here is what the app stores and when it may contact external services.

⬇

What is downloaded

Optional offline voices and learning lessons are downloaded on request. Some builds also allow a caretaker to import a compatible language model. Legal pages may be fetched from a configured host.

📱

What stays on the device

Symbols, messages, custom photos, and settings stay on the device during ordinary app use. Installed offline voices run locally.

🔒

What the app does not send

The app has no in-app analytics, advertising SDKs, or background sync. Optional downloads and legal pages send standard request metadata to their hosts.

🗑

What uninstalling removes

Uninstalling removes local app data. Exported backups and platform backups remain at their saved destinations until separately deleted.

Accessibility

Accessibility matrix

An honest look at what works today and where the limits are. Communication apps should be evaluated on access, not just features.

Status
Notes
Large tap targets
Supported
3-column grid uses tap targets at least 88 dp wide. Adjustable in caretaker settings.
Color-coded symbol borders
Supported
Modified Fitzgerald Key implemented across the entire vocabulary.
Offline operation
Supported
The core board and installed offline voices work without a network connection; downloaded lessons play offline.
TalkBack / screen reader
Supported
Every button, tile and option has a spoken name and state, and spoken messages are announced. Not yet tested by people who use TalkBack every day.
External keyboard
Supported
Tab and the arrow keys move between controls, Enter or Space presses them, Escape closes pop-ups, and a focus ring marks the active control. Drawing on the whiteboard still needs touch.
Kiosk / lockdown mode
Partial
Caretaker lock blocks settings; full Android kiosk pinning is set up externally.
Switch / eye-gaze access
Not yet
Not built in yet. Sono works with touch, an external keyboard or TalkBack.

Updates

What's new and what's next

Recent

  • 2026-11 Feature

    TalkBack and keyboard access

    Every button, tile and option in the app now has a spoken name for TalkBack and can be reached with an external keyboard. Escape closes pop-ups.

  • 2026-11 Feature

    Downloadable learning lessons

    Every lesson turns one word into a five-scene narrated story with illustrations and a short quiz. Lessons download on demand and can be replayed offline, so the app itself stays small.

  • 2026-04 Voice

    Offline neural voices in 10 languages

    Download a high-quality voice for your language once; after that it speaks without an internet connection. Croatian uses the device's own voice for now.

  • 2026-04 Feature

    Custom photos and recorded voices for any symbol

    Caretakers can replace any card with a personal photo and record a familiar voice, which helps with high-recognition vocabulary.

  • 2026-03 Privacy

    Caretaker lock and message history

    Settings sit behind a quick maths check, and the last 20 spoken sentences stay on the device for caretakers to review.

Roadmap

  • Switch and eye-gaze access

    Investigating switch scanning and head-tracking input for people who can't use touch or a keyboard.

  • iOS version

    An iPhone and iPad version is planned; packaging the offline voices for iOS is the main open task.

  • Printable caretaker quick-start

    A one-page PDF caretakers can print and keep next to the device from day one.

  • Searchable in-app help

    Bring the website guide's search into the app so caretakers can find answers without leaving it.

Roadmap items are intentions, not commitments. Priorities shift as we hear from people using the app.
