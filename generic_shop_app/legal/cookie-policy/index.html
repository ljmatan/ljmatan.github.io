Cookie Policy — Sono AAC Communicator

Last updated: 18 May 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website or web-based service to store information between sessions. They are a web browser technology and are not inherently part of native mobile applications.

2. Does This App Use Cookies?

Mostly no. Sono is a native mobile application. During normal use — browsing symbols, building sentences, playing voice output, recording optional custom voice messages, or using local sentence polishing — no web browser cookies, web sessions, or browser storage are used.

The App includes an embedded WebView component used only to display legal documents (this Cookie Policy, the Privacy Policy, and the Terms and Conditions) when you choose to view them. Depending on how those documents are hosted, that WebView may or may not transmit cookies to your device (see Section 4).

There are no tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies used at any point during normal operation of the App.

3. Local Storage Used by the App

Although the App does not use browser cookies during normal operation, it does store app settings and user-generated communication content on your device using the App’s internal cache and file storage. These local entries are used to operate the App and do not track you across applications or devices.

The App’s consent system may classify some local entries as mandatory for core AAC operation and others as functional preferences. If functional consent is not enabled, features that depend on functional local storage may be unavailable or may not retain their settings. Mandatory local storage remains necessary for the App to provide the configured communication board and user-created content.

4. Embedded WebView for Legal Document Display

The App includes an embedded WebView component (powered by webview_flutter) used exclusively to display legal documents — this Cookie Policy, the Privacy Policy, and the Terms and Conditions. This WebView is opened only when you deliberately tap a legal document link on the App’s welcome screen.

If the legal documents are hosted at a remote URL: The server delivering those documents may set cookies or use other tracking mechanisms in the WebView, in the same way as any website visited in a browser. In that case, the privacy and cookie practices of that hosting service apply — not this policy.

If the legal documents are served from local App assets: No external network request is made, and no cookies are set or received.

No other WebView component, embedded browser, or third-party web content is loaded during any other part of the App’s normal operation.

5. Third-Party Services and Their Own Storage

When you voluntarily download optional TTS model files or open externally hosted legal documents, the App makes HTTPS requests to third-party servers. These servers may set their own cookies or use their own tracking mechanisms if you subsequently visit them in a browser, but they do not set browser cookies on your device through the App’s native interface.

6. Removing All App Storage

You can remove all data stored by the App at any time by uninstalling the App from your device. This will permanently delete preference files, custom symbols, custom photos, custom voice recordings, message history, and downloaded voice model files. The process varies slightly by platform:

iOS: Go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) > Sono > Delete App.

Android: Go to Settings > Apps > Sono > Uninstall. Alternatively, use Settings > Apps > Sono > Storage > Clear Data to remove all stored data without uninstalling the App.

You can also remove individual custom photos, custom voice recordings, and other custom symbol content from the relevant app controls.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision. Because this App does not use browser cookies and its local storage practices change only with significant App updates, changes to this policy are expected to be infrequent.
