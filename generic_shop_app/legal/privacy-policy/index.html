Privacy Policy — Sono AAC Communicator

Last updated: 18 May 2026

1. Introduction and Identity of the Data Controller

Sono is a picture-symbol communication app designed to help people express themselves using images and spoken words.

Data Controller: The entity responsible for the processing of personal data in connection with this app is:

Matan Ljubas 10A Ul. Joze Krstića, Zadar, Zadarska županija 23000, Croatia Contact: lj.matan@gmail.com

EU Representative: The publisher is established in Croatia, within the European Economic Area. No separate EU representative is appointed for this app.

The core principle: Sono is designed to run on your device. It does not create user accounts, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not transmit your symbol board activity, custom content, recordings, or communication history to our servers.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Sono stores app settings and user-generated communication content locally on your device. This data does not leave the device through the App unless you use an operating-system backup, device migration, file-sharing, or cloud backup service (such as iCloud or Google Drive), which is governed by that service’s own privacy policy.

DataWhat It IsWhere Stored

Message history is stored locally for quick re-use and can be cleared from the relevant app controls or by deleting the App’s data. It is not transmitted to our servers.

3. How We Use This Information

The information listed above is used solely to operate the app on your device:

  • Display and board settings — adapt the communication board to the user’s needs.
  • Voice selection — drives the text-to-speech engine.
  • Custom content and media — displays personalised symbols, photos, labels, and voice recordings.
  • Activity history — supports recents, message history, and faster re-use of common communication.

None of this information is used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app locally on your device.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 applies, the legal bases under Article 6 GDPR for processing the data described in Section 2 are:

DataLegal BasisExplanation

5. Sentence Polishing

Sono uses a deterministic, rule-based grammar engine to make selected symbol sequences easier to speak. This processing happens locally on your device:

  • No on-device large language model is currently used for sentence polishing.
  • No sentences, symbols, or other communication input are sent to an external server for sentence polishing.
  • No profiling, automated decision-making, or inferences about any individual are produced by the sentence-polishing feature.

6. Text-to-Speech

Sono offers two text-to-speech modes:

Offline voices (preferred): The app can download language-specific voice models from a public repository (Sherpa-ONNX / GitHub releases). Once downloaded, all speech synthesis happens entirely on your device with no data transmitted externally.

Device system voices (fallback): When an offline voice is not available for the selected language, the app uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech service (provided by Apple or Google, depending on your device). These system services are governed by the privacy policies of Apple and Google respectively, and may transmit spoken text to their servers to synthesise it. You can avoid this by downloading an offline voice in Settings > Voice.

7. Optional Internet Connections

Sono has no mandatory internet connection. The app operates fully offline after installation.

The only network activity that occurs during normal use is when you choose to download optional resources or view externally hosted legal documents:

  • Offline voice models — downloaded from Sherpa-ONNX releases on GitHub (github.com) when you select and download a voice in Settings. Model size varies by language.
  • Legal documents — opened from the document URLs configured in the App.

These requests transfer only the data inherent to an HTTPS request, such as your IP address and basic request headers. No communication content, custom content, or usage data is included in these requests. Once a voice model is downloaded, speech synthesis with that model operates locally.

8. Device Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions:

PermissionWhy It Is Requested

The app does not request access to location, contacts, Bluetooth, health data, or any other sensitive permissions beyond those listed above.

9. Information We Do Not Collect

Sono does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our servers:

  • Communication content, custom photos, custom recordings, or symbol history
  • Usage patterns, session duration, or frequency of use
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprinting data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs sent to a remote server
  • Names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers beyond those entered locally by the caretaker
  • Location data of any kind
  • Biometric or health-related data

There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking libraries integrated into the app.

11. International Data Transfers

The app itself does not transfer your communication content to our infrastructure. However, when you voluntarily download TTS model files or open externally hosted legal documents, those requests are sent to servers operated by GitHub, which may process request metadata in the United States or other countries.

These requests do not contain communication content, custom photos, custom recordings, or user preferences. They contain only the standard information included in an HTTPS request, such as IP address and request headers.

Where GDPR applies, transfers of personal data to the United States rely on:

  • The EU–US Data Privacy Framework (where the recipient is certified), or
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as adopted by the European Commission, or
  • The fact that no personal data within the meaning of GDPR is transmitted in the relevant requests, rendering Chapter V GDPR inapplicable to those transfers.

For the processing carried out by Apple TTS and Google TTS (where used as fallback), please refer to Apple’s and Google’s respective privacy policies and their published transfer mechanisms.

12. Children’s Privacy

Sono is designed to support people of all ages who benefit from picture-symbol communication, including children.

Because the app does not transmit communication content to our servers and stores communication content locally on the device, it presents a reduced privacy risk for users, including children.

EU/EEA — Age of Digital Consent (GDPR Article 8): In the European Union and European Economic Area, the age at which a person can consent to data processing related to digital services varies by member state (between 13 and 16 years). Where the app is used by a child below the applicable age of digital consent, the consent of the holder of parental responsibility is required for consent-based processing. For this app, consent-based processing includes custom symbols, custom labels, custom photos, and custom voice recordings (see Section 4). Parents and guardians are encouraged to review and configure these settings.

If you choose to download a TTS voice model, that download occurs over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Parents and guardians who wish to prevent internet access by the app can do so by restricting internet access at the device or router level — the app will continue to function using the device’s available speech voices and any previously downloaded offline voices.

California (CCPA/CPRA): California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete personal information, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. Other US states: Residents of other states with applicable privacy laws have equivalent rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal information we hold. We do not hold personal information about app users on our servers. To exercise any rights or ask questions, contact us at the details in Section 19.

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Activity history: Recent symbols and message history are retained locally for quick re-use until cleared through app controls, cleared by the consent/storage system where applicable, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

Preference data (display, board, speech, and behaviour settings): Retained on the device until the caretaker changes the setting manually, clears app data, or uninstalls the app.

Custom content and media: Custom symbols, categories, labels, photos, and voice recordings are retained on the device until individually removed in the app, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

TTS model files: Retained on the device until deleted via Settings, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

To delete all app data: Uninstalling the app from your device removes locally stored files, including preferences, custom symbols, custom labels, custom photos, custom voice recordings, activity history, and downloaded voice model files.

We do not retain any of this information on our own servers, as none of it is ever transmitted to us.

16. Data Security

All data stored by the app resides in the app’s private sandboxed container on your device, which is protected by your operating system’s built-in security mechanisms (file-system encryption, process isolation, app sandboxing). The app does not implement encryption layers beyond what the operating system provides.

Because no personal data is transmitted to our infrastructure, there is no server-side data held by us that could be subject to a breach on our end. In the unlikely event we become aware of a security vulnerability in the app that affects personal data, we will notify affected users through an app update and, where required by law, notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware (GDPR Article 33).

17. Caretaker Settings Access

Settings (including the ability to change voice, board settings, custom symbols, custom photos, and custom recordings) are accessible via a hidden gesture — four taps within two seconds on the sentence area. This mechanism is intended as a simple barrier to accidental changes, not as a strong access control system. If you require strict access control, we recommend using your device’s guided access or screen time features to restrict in-app navigation.

Continued use of the app after a change is published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy, unless your continued use is on the basis of a right that requires a fresh consent, in which case we will seek that consent explicitly.
